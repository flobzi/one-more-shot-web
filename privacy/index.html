One More Shot: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

One More Shot ("the game") is published by Sweid Group SARL, Lebanon ("we", "us"). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL: to be added].

There are no accounts. You never sign in, and we do not ask for your email, phone number, real name or location.

Saved on your device

Your level progress, coins, shop items, settings, chosen palette, best scores, the player name you type and a random device token are kept in your browser's local storage (on the app versions, in the app's own storage). Clearing the site data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, deletes it. Nothing here is sent anywhere except what is listed under "Online leaderboard".

Online leaderboard (PVP Ranked)

When you finish a PVP Ranked run, the game sends it to our leaderboard service, which runs on Cloudflare (a Cloudflare Worker with a Cloudflare D1 database). For each run we store:

  • the player name you chose and your random device token (16 random bytes made once per browser; it is not linked to your identity and is never shown to others);
  • the score and run statistics (makes, swishes, shots, best streak), the game build and the time;
  • the run's input log (the timing and finger positions of your shots), so the server can replay the run and check that the score is genuine;
  • a one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself), used only to limit how many runs can be submitted per hour.

We do not store your user-agent or your IP address. The public leaderboard shows only player names, scores and ranks. Runs that fail the checks are kept hidden for review. Renaming yourself updates your stored name. We keep this data for as long as the leaderboard runs; to have your runs deleted, contact us with your player name.

Hosting

The game's files are served by GitHub Pages and through Cloudflare. Like any website, these providers process your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages and protect against abuse, under their own privacy policies (GitHub, Cloudflare). We do not use analytics, tracking cookies or third-party trackers.

Advertising

The web game currently shows only labelled test placeholders in its ad spaces; no ad network is loaded and no advertising data is collected. If we enable real ads (Google AdSense) on the web, Google may then collect device identifiers, IP address and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads (how Google uses this), and where the law requires it you will be asked for consent first. We will update this page before that happens.

Android app. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens, never while you are shooting. To deliver and measure these ads Google collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), and how you interact with the ads and the app (how Google uses this). Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before the first ad is requested, and you can change your answer at any time under SETTINGS > AD CHOICES. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

You can delete everything stored on your device yourself (see above). For leaderboard data, contact us to access or delete it. We do not sell personal information.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
